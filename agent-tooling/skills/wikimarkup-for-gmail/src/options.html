WikiMarkup for Gmail

Your wiki, your signature and your timestamp style, then Gmail understands wikitext the way a wiki does.

1 · Your home wiki
2 · Your signature
3 · Timestamp

The time in ~~~~ and ~~~~~, set up like MediaWiki's own preferences. Worked out in your browser — nothing is looked up online.

Behaviour
Permission

Only markup you type in a Gmail draft counts — never quoted or forwarded mail, never what was already there. When you pause (or leave the message body), a popup next to the compose box lists what it would convert: Convert, Convert all in this draft, or Not in this draft.

Hotkeys inside a draft (Alt = Option on a Mac): Alt+Shift+W ask now · Alt+Shift+S signature · Alt+Shift+T timestamp · Alt+Shift+P paste as wikitext. Paste as wikitext sends the copied HTML to your home wiki for conversion; nothing else leaves the browser.
